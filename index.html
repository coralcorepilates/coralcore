About us

Inspired by the coral reef

Coral Core Pilates is a Pilates studio and movement community founded by Karla Deida in Mayagüez, Puerto Rico, inspired by the strength, resilience, and interconnectedness of coral reef ecosystems.

Our approach is rooted in contemporary Pilates, respecting the principles and purpose of the method while incorporating our evolving understanding of the human body, movement, and biomechanics. Our practice begins with your center: through intentional movement, we develop strength, stability, mobility, posture, balance, and body awareness as a connected system.

Meet Karla

Karla trained through The Lolita's Legacy™ Teacher Training Program under Maria Teresa Robles, completing 408 hours of Pilates training, and brings over 300 hours of teaching experience. Her training blends the classical Pilates method with a contemporary approach, incorporating ballet-inspired movements to create mindful, dynamic, and intentional movement experiences.

Karla is committed to continuing education. In September 2026 she completed “A Contrology Experience,” a 3-day immersive training in the traditional Pilates method with Classic Pilates PR.

Frequently asked questions

Which class should I choose?
  • Contemporary Mat Pilates – Basic is the best place to start if you are new to Pilates or want to build a strong foundation.
  • Contemporary Mat Class – All Levels is a more dynamic class open to everyone, with options to make each exercise easier or more challenging.
How do I book a class?
  • All bookings are made through Punchpass. Create your free account, choose a class, and use a pass or buy one at checkout.
  • Need help? Watch our step-by-step booking tutorial on Instagram.
Do I need experience?
  • No experience needed. Your instructor guides you through every movement and adapts to your pace.
  • New to the studio? Try the Intro Offer: 2 mat classes for $30.
Is Pilates for everyone? Is there a gender or age limit?
  • Pilates is for everybody. The method was created by Joseph Pilates, and many of his first students were men, including boxers and athletes. Today it builds strength, mobility, and balance for every body.
  • Our classes welcome people of every gender and every age, body type, and fitness level, whether you are a man, a woman, or however you identify. Everyone is welcome here, and each exercise can be adapted to you.
  • Participants under 18 need written authorization from a parent or legal guardian.
What should I wear and bring?
  • Wear comfortable, fitted clothing that lets you move freely.
  • Grip socks are recommended but not required.
  • Bring a towel (required) and water. Forgot your towel? You can rent one for $2.
How many people are in each class?
  • Group classes have a maximum of 8 people, so you get personal attention in an intimate, welcoming space.
What if I have an injury or am pregnant?
  • Let your instructor know before class about any injuries, health conditions, or pregnancy so she can offer modifications.
  • If you are pregnant, recovering from surgery, or managing an injury, check with your doctor before starting. A private session can be a great way to begin.

Health readiness questionnaire

Is Pilates right for me?

Pilates is safe for most people, and this short questionnaire helps you check whether you can start right away or should talk to your doctor or to the studio first. It takes about 3 minutes. Answer each question with Yes or No based on how you feel today.

Please note: This questionnaire is a self-check, not a medical evaluation. It does not replace advice from your doctor or another healthcare provider. If you are ever unsure, check with your provider before starting any new exercise program.

Your answers are private. They stay on your device and are not sent or saved anywhere. You do not need to share them with anyone, but telling your instructor about any condition or injury helps her adapt the class safely for you.

Section 1: General health

A Yes in this section means you should get your doctor's okay before your first class.

1

2

3

4

5

6

Studio rules & requirements
  • Bring your own towel to every class.
  • Grip socks are recommended for safety and stability, but are not required.
  • Bring water and stay hydrated throughout your session.
  • Arrive 5–10 minutes early to allow enough time to get settled.
  • Late entry is not permitted after the first 15 minutes of class. Please arrive on time out of respect for your safety, your instructor, and your fellow clients.
  • Clean your mat and props after each class.
  • Respect the studio space and your fellow clients.
  • Coral Core Pilates reserves the right of admission.
  • For the full summary, see our Client Guide (PDF).
Parking
  • Free parking is available on the Val Harbour Building premises.
  • Some spots are reserved for other businesses and offices in the building. Before parking, please make sure your vehicle is not in one of these reserved spots.
  • Respecting our neighbors' parking helps us keep a good relationship with everyone in the building. Thank you!
Cancellation policy
  • Cancel or reschedule at least 8 hours before class to avoid losing your session.
  • Late cancellations and no-shows are non-refundable.
  • In the event of an extraordinary or unforeseen circumstance, please contact the studio via WhatsApp as soon as possible. Exceptions may be considered on a case-by-case basis.
Towel policy
  • For hygiene and studio cleanliness, bringing a towel to every class is required.
  • If you forget or do not have a towel, a studio towel rental is available for $2 per towel, subject to availability.
  • Towels will also be available for purchase while supplies last.
Minimum class attendance
  • A minimum of 2 participants is required for a class to take place.
  • If the minimum is not reached, Coral Core Pilates may cancel or reschedule the class. Clients with reservations will be notified and may transfer to another class or receive an alternative option.
  • Studio-initiated cancellations never count as a late cancellation or no-show, and you won't lose a class credit.
Waitlist policy
  • Joining a waitlist does not deduct a class from your package or membership.
  • When a spot opens, you'll receive an email notification through Punchpass. You must then register for the class to secure your spot.
  • Being notified of an available spot does not automatically reserve your place in the class.

Meet Karla

Training & education

Rooted in the classical lineage of Joseph Pilates and always learning.

01
Comprehensive teacher training

The Lolita's Legacy™ Teacher Training Program

A 500-hour comprehensive Pilates teacher training created by Pilates Elder Lolita San Miguel, born in New York to Puerto Rican parents and one of the only living master teachers directly certified by Joseph Pilates. The curriculum blends classical Joseph Pilates work with modern biomechanics and movement science, and is recognized by the Pilates Method Alliance (PMA) / National Pilates Certification Program.

Karla trained under Maria Teresa Robles, who completed the Lolita San Miguel Pilates Master Mentor Program (PMMP), a two-year, 160-hour advanced program, directly with Lolita.

What guides us

Mission & vision

Our mission

At Coral Core Pilates, our mission is to create a welcoming, inclusive, and judgment-free space where every body feels seen, respected, and comfortable moving exactly as they are.

We believe Pilates is more than a workout. It is an opportunity to build a deeper connection between the mind and body through intentional movement, mindful practice, and an understanding of how your body moves.

Our teaching is grounded in form, posture, alignment, and safety over competitiveness. We encourage each client to move with intention, offering modifications and guidance when needed so that every person can experience Pilates in a way that feels safe, effective, and meaningful to them.

We honor and respect the Pilates method as developed by Joseph Pilates, recognizing the principles, structure, and intention behind the method. At the same time, we believe that our understanding of the human body, movement, and biomechanics continues to evolve. Contemporary Pilates allows us to build upon this foundation with current knowledge while preserving the integrity and purpose of the method. As our knowledge of movement evolves, so does our ability to teach with greater awareness and intention.

Because no two bodies are the same, and no two bodies move the same way, Pilates will feel different for everyone. Our role is not to make your body fit the movement, but to help you understand your body, find the movement that works for you, and ultimately enjoy the process.

Our vision

Our vision is to build a strong and connected Pilates community where everyone feels that they belong.

We strive to create a space that feels like it belongs to all of us—a space where people can come without judgment, support one another, celebrate progress, and feel encouraged to pursue their own goals at their own pace.

Our studio is a sacred space. Our people come first. We are committed to cultivating a community rooted in inclusion, respect, connection, and genuine care, while continuing to grow and evolve together.

Whether your goal is to become stronger, move with greater ease, improve your posture, reconnect with your body, challenge yourself, or simply take time for yourself, there is a place for you here.

Come as you are. Move with intention. Leave feeling more connected to yourself and your community.
