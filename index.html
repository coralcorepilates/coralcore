About us

Inspired by the coral reef

Coral Core Pilates is a Pilates studio and movement community founded by Karla Deida in Mayagüez, Puerto Rico, inspired by the strength, resilience, and interconnectedness of coral reef ecosystems.

Our approach is rooted in contemporary Pilates, respecting the principles and purpose of the method while incorporating our evolving understanding of the human body, movement, and biomechanics. Our practice begins with your center: through intentional movement, we develop strength, stability, mobility, posture, balance, and body awareness as a connected system.

Meet Karla

An instructor in training with over 200 hours of teaching experience, currently completing the Lolita's Legacy Teacher Training Program. Her training blends the classical Pilates method with a contemporary approach, incorporating ballet-inspired movements to create mindful, dynamic, and intentional movement experiences.

Good to know

Studio policies

Studio rules & requirements
  • Bring your own towel to every class.
  • Grip socks are recommended for safety and stability, but are not required.
  • Bring water and stay hydrated throughout your session.
  • Arrive 5–10 minutes early to allow enough time to get settled.
  • Late entry is not permitted after the first 15 minutes of class. Please arrive on time out of respect for your safety, your instructor, and your fellow clients.
  • Clean your mat and props after each class.
  • Respect the studio space and your fellow clients.
  • Coral Core Pilates reserves the right of admission.
Cancellation policy
  • Cancel or reschedule at least 8 hours before class to avoid losing your session.
  • Late cancellations and no-shows are non-refundable.
  • In the event of an extraordinary or unforeseen circumstance, please contact the studio via WhatsApp as soon as possible. Exceptions may be considered on a case-by-case basis.
Towel policy
  • For hygiene and studio cleanliness, bringing a towel to every class is required.
  • If you forget or do not have a towel, a studio towel rental is available for $2 per towel, subject to availability.
  • Towels will also be available for purchase while supplies last.
Minimum class attendance
  • A minimum of 2 participants is required for a class to take place.
  • If the minimum is not reached, Coral Core Pilates may cancel or reschedule the class. Clients with reservations will be notified and may transfer to another class or receive an alternative option.
  • Studio-initiated cancellations never count as a late cancellation or no-show, and you won't lose a class credit.
Waitlist policy
  • Joining a waitlist does not deduct a class from your package or membership.
  • When a spot opens, you'll receive an email notification through Punchpass. You must then register for the class to secure your spot.
  • Being notified of an available spot does not automatically reserve your place in the class.
